Gym Book Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Gym Book keeps everything you log on your own iPhone. We don't collect, store or share any of your data, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

Who we are

Gym Book is made by Allen Bastow, an independent developer. You can contact us at allen.d.bastow@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Everything you enter in Gym Book stays on your device: your exercises, workouts, sets, goals, body weight entries and settings. This data is stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Gym Book does not collect personal data, does not use analytics or advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no third-party code that does.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Gym Book uses them only to tell you when your rest timer ends. These notifications are created on your phone. Nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn them off at any time in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Gym Book.

Exporting your data

Settings › Export data creates spreadsheet files of your workouts and body weight. The files are only shared where you choose to send or save them (for example Files, AirDrop or email).

Backups

Gym Book data is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Test versions (TestFlight)

If you use a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, basic device information and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with us, so we can fix problems. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy and is only used to improve Gym Book.

Deleting your data

Because your data only exists on your device, you're in full control. You can delete individual workouts or entries in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app.

Children

Gym Book is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

We don't hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions about your privacy, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If Gym Book changes how it handles data (for example, if we add optional iCloud sync), we will update this page and the date at the top before that change is released.
